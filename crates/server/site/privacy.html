Privacy policy

Last updated September 25, 2026

Your recipes

Recipes, notes and grocery lists are stored on your device. If you use iCloud, Apple syncs them between your devices under your Apple ID. They are never sent to us.

When you import a recipe from a link, your device downloads that page directly from the website. We are not involved.

Usage statistics (off unless you turn them on)

Hestia has an optional setting to share anonymous usage statistics. It is off by default. If you turn it on and also choose to send the statistics, the app sends us, at most once a day:

  • how many times certain things happened since the last report, such as the app being opened, a recipe being created or imported, or the screen being kept on while cooking;
  • the app version and platform (for example "ios");
  • a random identifier that is created when you turn statistics on and discarded when you turn them off.

Reports never contain recipe content, links, search terms, names, email addresses, contacts, location, or advertising identifiers. The Settings screen in the app shows exactly what would be sent. We use the statistics only to understand which features are used, we do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not sell or share the data.

Turning the setting off stops collection and deletes the unsent counts on your device.
