Privacy policy

Last updated October 24, 2026

Your recipes

Recipes, notes and grocery lists are stored on your device. If you use iCloud, Apple syncs them between your devices under your Apple ID. They are not sent to us, unless you share a recipe as described below.

When you import a recipe from a link, your device downloads that page directly from the website. We are not involved, unless the link is a Hestia share link.

Sharing a recipe

When you tap Share on a recipe and confirm Create link, the app uploads that one recipe to our server so it can be opened from a link. Nothing is uploaded until you confirm. The upload contains:

  • the title, description, author, servings or yield, prep, cook and total times, ingredients, steps and tags;
  • the link to the website the recipe came from and the link to its photo, if it has them.

It does not contain your notes, photo files stored on your device, whether the recipe is a favorite, your other recipes, or anything that identifies you or your device. The stored link is not tied to your device, your statistics identifier, or any account.

Anyone who has the link can view the recipe and import it, so only send it to people you want to have it. Shared pages ask search engines not to index them. A link shows the recipe as it was when you shared it and is deleted from our server 90 days after it was created. To have a link removed sooner, contact us with the link.

A shared page that includes a photo link loads that photo from the website hosting it, so that website may see the visit.

Usage statistics (off unless you turn them on)

Hestia has an optional setting to share anonymous usage statistics. It is off by default. If you turn it on and also choose to send the statistics, the app sends us, at most once a day:

  • how many times certain things happened since the last report, such as the app being opened, a recipe being created, imported or shared, or cook mode being started or finished;
  • the app version and platform (for example "ios");
  • a random identifier that is created when you turn statistics on and discarded when you turn them off.

Reports never contain recipe content, links, search terms, names, email addresses, contacts, location, or advertising identifiers. The Settings screen in the app shows exactly what would be sent. We use the statistics only to understand which features are used, we do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not sell or share the data.

Turning the setting off stops collection and deletes the unsent counts on your device.
